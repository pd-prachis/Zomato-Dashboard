📌 Project Overview:

In the hyper-competitive food delivery industry, understanding customer preferences, restaurant performance, and market trends is key to growth. This Zomato Data Analysis Dashboard offers a comprehensive business intelligence solution that explores patterns across cuisine types, cities, ratings, delivery modes, and price ranges—crafted to guide strategic decisions in food-tech.

Designed for:


Business Analysts to evaluate food trends and consumer behavior.

Marketing Teams to identify city-wise and cuisine-wise opportunities.

Zomato Stakeholders to monitor performance across markets.

🛠️ Project Workflow:

Step 1: Imported raw Zomato dataset into Power BI Desktop.

Step 2: Cleaned and pre-processed data using Power Query Editor:

Removed duplicate/incomplete entries.

Standardized values across rating categories and cuisines.

Handled nulls and transformed data types.

Step 3: Modeled data for relational insights and built DAX measures for:

Total Restaurants by City, Cuisine, and Rating

Votes Distribution by Rating Category

Maximum Average Cost for Two

Cuisine Popularity Analysis

Delivery Preferences (Online / Dine-in)

Step 4: Created engaging and insightful visuals:

Pie & Bar Charts to highlight top cuisines and city dominance

Stacked Bars to compare delivery modes

Line Graphs for cost analysis trends

Rating vs Votes Analysis using color-coded visualizations

Step 5: Published the dashboard on Power BI Service for interactive and secure access.

📊 Key Visual Insights:

🏙️ Restaurant Distribution by City
New Delhi dominates with 60%+ share, followed by Gurgaon and Noida.

Cities like Mumbai, Chennai, and Bangalore show growing presence.

🍱 Popular Cuisines:

North Indian (35.5%) and Chinese (29.8%) are most popular.

Continental, Mughlai, and Italian cuisines are also widely available.

🌟 Rating Analysis:

Majority rated as Good (40%) and Very Good (27%)

Only 2.2% marked as Poor, indicating overall quality consistency.

💸 Cost and Voting Metrics:

Visuals showing votes vs. average cost for two, helping identify value-for-money zones.

Votes by Rating Category to assess customer satisfaction trends.

🔍 Business Insights:

High concentration of restaurants in urban hubs reflects regional demand surges.

Cuisine data uncovers market preferences and potential gaps.

Online delivery dominates over dine-in, aligning with post-pandemic shifts.

Restaurants with higher costs do not always equate to higher ratings—suggesting experience and quality are key.

Cities like Pune, Kochi, and Coimbatore represent emerging food-tech markets.
